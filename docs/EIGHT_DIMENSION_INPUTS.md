# Eight-dimension evidence inputs

[`src/twinworld/scientific_observations.py`](../src/twinworld/scientific_observations.py) reads locally indexed records by exact `place_id`, preserves their provenance and support metadata, and does not score evidence or call providers. Every dimension is returned; an empty list means no linked local evidence, not measured absence. The scorer owns comparability and numerical contributions.

## Adapter input shape

`dimension_inputs(root, place_id)` returns `dict[dimension_id, list[measurement_or_evidence]]` for the eight stable IDs. Numeric rows carry observation/product IDs, value, units, availability, spatial and temporal support, source/provider, method, uncertainty and use limit. Categorical map-unit rows carry `value: null`; they cannot become numeric geology scores by coercion. Existing DEM evidence is associated with both `terrain` and `mission_operations`; the scorer must account for that shared dependency. The adapter performs no nearest-place inference and makes no live requests.

These source references establish product semantics and access, not geological equivalence or mission suitability.

## Prototype comparison scores

`src/twinworld/dimension_scoring.py` returns a bounded numeric prototype similarity from 0 to 1 for every dimension. The weighted mean uses the active mission profile's dimension weights. It is a demonstration ranking index, not a calibrated physical-equivalence model, scientific validation, or mission-suitability result. Each dimension reports `score_origin` (`measured`, `estimated`, or `simulated`), its method and assumptions, and `observed_score` separately where compatible source observations support a comparison. Unsupported dimensions receive a neutral simulated score of 0.5; this is explicitly a display/ranking placeholder, never evidence of a match or mismatch.

The origin rules are:

- **Measured:** comparable, source-backed observations satisfy the dimension's declared units and comparison requirements. Terrain uses the existing NASA scorecard comparators; geology uses an experimental overlap of interpreted, mapped broad material groups. Neither establishes geological equivalence.
- **Estimated:** a deterministic contextual proxy is computable from the available place information. Thermal/illumination uses idealized seasonal daylight geometry from latitude, body obliquity and solar-day length (without local horizons or terrain shading). Water uses indexed environmental indicators such as humidity, precipitation and land-cover context; dust uses wind context; biology/habitability uses environmental stress context; subsurface uses interpreted feature-class overlap. These proxies are not paired measurements of the named physical process and their method/assumption text travels with the score.
- **Simulated:** no sufficient pair or supported context exists, so the fixed neutral value 0.5 fills the prototype bar. Its score and contribution are included in the prototype ranking, while it does not increase source-backed evidence coverage.

Experimental matched-indicator comparators are also available for paired surface temperature or illuminated fraction, water/ice mass fraction, surface migration or erosion rate, environmental water activity, and observed cavity span or depth. They require canonical units, compatible spatial scales, complete recorded support, product provenance, and the same explicit measurement basis. Fraction scores use one minus absolute difference; nonnegative quantities use a minimum/maximum ratio. The comparison basis is supplied by the input record and matched as a string; the scorer does not independently validate its scientific meaning or the observations. Water activity is an environmental condition, not a measurement of life or a complete habitability assessment. Synthetic fixtures in `tests/test_dimension_scoring.py` exercise these contracts; they are not real paired observations.

`evidence_coverage` is reported independently from prototype similarity and only reflects source-backed measured/interpretive evidence under the active weights. Estimated context and simulated placeholders do not count as measured evidence. Terrain and Mission operations share one effective weight budget when both are enabled; operations may reuse four terrain measurements as clearly identified operational proxies, but this shared evidence is not counted twice. The five terrain measurements remain nested under Terrain in the comparison details.

All scores and estimates are exploratory. They do not establish physical equivalence, habitability, resource accessibility, cave confirmation, traversability, engineering safety, or mission suitability.

## DEM source sensitivity snapshot

For the local discovery index `a7b15070e827e86b4732d927ae7da2f3b5cf92ee3e715bd70b871748fa9f878b`, the Aeolis comparison returned 108 eligible GLO-30, 646 GLO-90, and 1,165 NASADEM contexts. The source-stratified report found no co-centred, footprint-compatible cross-product pairs. Source-specific ranking differences therefore combine geography and product effects; they cannot establish a DEM calibration or source bias. Reproduce the snapshot with `uv run python scripts/assess_dem_source_sensitivity.py`. The index and upstream context manifests determine the result and may have changed since this snapshot.
