Pictures are illustrations: swap in real imagery such as NASA Earth Observatory / Landsat scenes for Earth, LROC (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera) images for the Moon, and HiRISE or CTX images for Mars. Similarity scores are the team's judgement for now and could later be calculated from datasets (elevation, temperature, mineralogy).
